Project 2 · Climate futures

Breadth: speculation in several genres addressing the same problem, climate change.

Tetrad wheel Corpus Initial readings

Scope

Speculations that a medium will cause, prevent, slow or adapt to climate change, or that imagine a world reshaped by it, coded with the same codebook. Each strand has its own selection rule, so that the corpus is not chosen to fit the readings of Project 1, and each is sampled across its own history rather than only the present. As in Project 1, installations, games and live performances are excluded for now.

Media, to be fixed from the corpus. Candidate families: energy · carbon removal and geoengineering · transport · food and land · buildings and cities · computing and data · policy instruments and markets · ways of living.

How it is developed

Academic speculative design. Soden et al. (2021b) reviewed papers in the ACM Digital Library from 2009 to 2021, including those where environmental sustainability is the primary issue and speculative design, or a related practice such as design fiction or critical design, is the primary method, by the authors’ own account. Of 171 papers, 40 met these criteria; they split them into 28 project papers, which describe and evaluate specific speculative works, and 12 programmatic papers on the role of speculative design in sustainability. I take the 28 project papers, since they contain the speculations themselves.

Open. Speculations with no medium, such as catastrophe alone, give the Tetrad nothing to read. To decide: exclude them and report how many, or code the catastrophe as what the world’s existing media reverse into.
