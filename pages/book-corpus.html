Scope

Speculations that a medium will transform or replace the book, reading, teaching or the school, from 1906 to the present, in any format. Excluded: speculations about media with no bearing on learning or reading, and works that cannot be quoted or described from a document, such as installations, games and live performances.

Media: stereograph · film · radio · television · teaching machine · computer · virtual reality · internet · AI · direct transmission to the brain. For colour, they are shown in seven families: stereograph and film · radio and television · teaching machines · computers · internet · AI · VR and direct transmission.

How it was developed

Pilot (31 speculations). Assembled purposively, not by systematic search: claims quoted in histories of educational technology (Cuban 1986; Watters 2021) and traced, where possible, to the press, broadcast and archival sources they cite; widely discussed fiction, film and images; and scenario and speculative design work on education (e.g. Bayne 2015; Selwyn et al. 2020; OECD 2020). Each claim records its source, and a to verify flag where the wording comes only from a secondary source.

Extension (in progress). I am building a corpus of speculative design papers on education through searches of the ACM Digital Library, run in October 2026. Six searches pair a method term with an education term, both in the title or both in the abstract:

  1. “speculative design” and “education”
  2. “speculative design” and “classroom”
  3. “speculative design” and “school”
  4. “design fiction” and “education”
  5. “design fiction” and “classroom”
  6. “design fiction” and “school”

Each result is screened on its abstract, and where needed its full text, against two criteria: (1) is education, learning or the school the primary issue the paper addresses? (2) is speculative design, or a related practice such as design fiction, the primary research method, according to the authors? Included papers are then split into project papers, which present specific speculations, and programmatic papers on the role of speculative design in education; only project papers are coded. Counts at each stage will be reported here.

Sources

Every speculation by the year it was made. Hover a dot for a summary and select it to find its source in the list. Search to filter both.

    Limitation

    A purposive pilot leans towards well-known, mostly American sources; the extension and its stated search procedure are meant to correct this.
