An Archive of Past Futures

Claims that a new medium would transform or replace the book in education, from the classroom stereoscope to AI tutors. Each claim sits in the Tetrad sector it promised (enhance, obsolesce, retrieve, reverse) at its speculative distance from its own present. Hatched cells are gaps: no claim imagined them. Open a gap to see the design fiction it calls for.

Coding scheme: Tetrad laws after McLuhan & McLuhan (1988). Each claim also records who is speaking. Speculative distance on construal-level theory's four dimensions of psychological distance (Trope & Liberman 2010): temporal, spatial, social and hypotheticality. Temporal distance is coded from the horizon a claim states (imminent, near, distant), since most give no year; “unstated” is a value in its own right. Construal level (concrete or abstract language) is coded as the kind of thinking distance is expected to produce. Scope of change (supplement, transform practice, replace the medium, replace the institution) is a dimension specific to obsolescence. Design-fiction storyboards are placeholders for AI-assisted animations.
